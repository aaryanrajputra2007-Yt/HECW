Production notes:

Look: Dark navy night palette. Starlight is always golden and hot/cold layers are always red vs blue, so students connect color with density without needing to read anything.
Audio: Soft ambient space hum at the start, a whoosh at the atmosphere entry, a subtle "ting" on every bright flicker in shot 6, and a bass drop on the "ATMOSPHERIC REFRACTION" stamp.
Captions: Burned-in Hinglish subtitles throughout. Put NCERT terms (atmosphere, refractive index, point, twinkling, atmospheric refraction) in bold yellow so they stick for exams.
Loop trick: The last frame (the flickering star) matches the first frame (a star in space), so the Short loops seamlessly and rewatches go up.
